INTRODUCTION

CYBER is a YSWS in which you design a custom cyberdeck, and we ship you a raspberry pi compute module (or any other :D), a custom PCB, and all your components so you can build it!

WHAT IS A CYBERDECK?

A cyberdeck is a portable computer that is designed for hacking and to be hacked. They are usually modular, and use a laptop motherboard or SBC (like a rpi CM) as the main processor. They often follow a cyberpunk or retrofuturistic design aesthetic, and have non-conventional displays or input devices.
